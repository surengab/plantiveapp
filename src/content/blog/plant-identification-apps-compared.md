## How the apps differ

### Plantive: focused identification and care

Plantive's App Store listing describes photo identification for plants, flowers, trees, succulents and houseplants, followed by watering, light and general care information. It is listed as a 15.3 MB iPhone app requiring iOS 17 or later.

The listing's developer-supplied privacy label says **Data Not Collected**. It does not currently list care reminders, a light meter, a plant journal or expert consultations. Choose it when a smaller, more focused identification-and-guidance app matches the job; do not choose it expecting an unlisted scheduling system.

### PictureThis: the broad identification toolkit

[PictureThis](https://www.picturethisai.com/) lists plant and weed identification, problem guidance, care reminders, a light meter, collection tools and expert consultation. Its App Store listing supports iPhone and iPad and requires iOS 15 or later.

That breadth may suit someone who wants many tools in one app. For a direct feature, compatibility and privacy-label comparison, read [Plantive vs PictureThis](/blog/plantive-vs-picturethis/).

### Planta: schedules and plant organization

[Planta](https://getplanta.com/) emphasizes individualized care schedules, reminders, a journal, light measurement, problem guidance and community features. Its current App Store listing requires iOS 18 or later and lists iPhone and iPad support.

It is the most obviously schedule-centered choice in this group based on the public listing. See [Plantive vs Planta](/blog/plantive-vs-planta/) for the focused comparison.

### Plantum: identification plus utility tools

[Plantum](https://myplantum.com/) lists identification across plants and other natural objects, along with reminders, meters, calculators, weather information and a journal. That makes it relevant to people who want more than houseplant identification.

Its App Store listing also describes multiple premium plans. Check which features are available in your region and plan before relying on the headline feature list.

### Blossom: guided care and growing features

[Blossom](https://blossomplant.com/) lists plant identification, reminders, problem guidance, a water calculator, a journal and tools for growing edible plants. It is another broad care suite rather than a narrowly focused identifier.

The right question is whether those additional tools solve a problem you have, not which listing contains the longest feature list.

## Privacy labels need context

Apple requires developers to describe their apps' data practices, but [Apple explains that these disclosures are developer-reported](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/). The App Store also notes that the information is not verified by Apple.

Plantive currently reports **Data Not Collected**. The other apps' labels list various data categories used for functionality, personalization, analytics or tracking. Open each current privacy label and the linked privacy policy before deciding; a category can cover different data and uses in different apps.

## Why there is no accuracy winner here

The listings make different identification claims, but they are marketing sources. This page does not use a shared, independent benchmark that tests all five apps on the same labelled photographs under the same conditions.

For any app, improve the input before judging the result:

1. Photograph a distinctive flower, leaf or fruit in focus.
2. Add the whole plant and its growth habit when possible.
3. Compare the suggested botanical name with a reliable reference.
4. Treat disagreement or a poor photo as uncertainty, not proof.
5. Never use one image result alone to decide whether a plant is edible or safe for a pet.

The [photo identification guide](/blog/how-to-identify-a-plant-from-a-photo/) provides a complete verification checklist.

## Which app should you try?

- **Try Plantive** when you want a focused iPhone identifier and care reference, a small listed download, and its current privacy-label approach matters to you.
- **Consider PictureThis** when weed identification, a light meter, reminders or listed expert consultation are important.
- **Consider Planta** when schedules, a journal and managing an established collection are the primary job.
- **Consider Plantum** when you want additional meters, calculators or broader natural-object identification.
- **Consider Blossom** when reminders, edible-growing tools and a broad care suite match your needs.

Download models and premium access vary, so inspect the purchase sheet before starting a trial or subscription. The most useful app is the one whose current features fit your routine and whose suggested identifications you still verify.
