## The main difference: lookup or routine

Plantive's listing describes a short path: photograph a plant, receive a suggested identification, then read watering, light and general care guidance. That is a lookup-oriented experience.

[Planta](https://getplanta.com/)'s listing emphasizes an ongoing routine. It describes schedules that account for the plant and environment, reminders for multiple care tasks, a journal, progress records and care sharing. It also lists a light meter and problem guidance.

If forgetting tasks is the central problem, Planta's advertised workflow is closer to that need. If you mainly want to identify a plant and open straightforward guidance, Plantive is the more focused option based on the current listings.

## Identification claims

Both apps list photo identification. Planta says it uses the identification to create a tailored care plan; Plantive says it follows identification with useful care information.

This page does not name an accuracy winner. The listings are developer marketing, not a controlled comparison using the same labelled photographs. Verify any suggested botanical name before changing care, handling an unknown plant or making a safety decision. Start with the [photo identification guide](/blog/how-to-identify-a-plant-from-a-photo/).

## Privacy-label differences

Plantive's current developer-reported App Store label says **Data Not Collected**. Planta's current label lists data used for tracking and data linked to the user, including categories used for app functionality, personalization, analytics or marketing.

[Apple explains that developers provide these privacy disclosures](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/); the App Store says Apple has not verified them. Review the detailed purposes and each app's privacy policy rather than treating this short summary as an audit.

## Compatibility, size and purchases

Plantive currently requires iOS 17 or later and is listed at 15.3 MB for iPhone. Planta currently requires iOS 18 or later, lists iPhone and iPad support, and is shown at 288.4 MB.

Both listings say free with in-app purchases. Planta currently presents subscription options; Plantive presents in-app purchase options. Exact availability, trials and prices vary by region and can change between versions.

## Which one fits you?

**Plantive may fit better if:**

- You primarily need photo identification followed by care guidance.
- You prefer a smaller, focused app rather than a full care organizer.
- Plantive's current privacy-label approach matters to you.
- Your iPhone runs iOS 17 but not iOS 18.

**Planta may fit better if:**

- You want schedules and reminders across a plant collection.
- You use a journal or progress photos to manage ongoing care.
- A light meter, shared care or community features matter.
- You want the currently listed iPad support.
